Privacy Policy

rclone Google Drive

This privacy policy explains how the rclone Google Drive application uses Google account information.

Information accessed

The application uses Google's OAuth authorization to access Google Drive on behalf of the authorized user. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include access to files and folders in Google Drive.

How information is used

Google Drive information is used solely to allow rclone to transfer, copy, and manage files between the user's local computer and Google Drive.

The application does not sell, rent, or use Google Drive information for advertising.

Storage and sharing

The application does not independently store or share Google account information with third parties. Files transferred through the application are stored in the Google Drive account selected by the user and on the user's local computer as applicable.

OAuth credentials are used to authenticate the application with Google and are not published on this website.

Data retention

The application does not retain Google Drive data on this website. Access can be revoked at any time through the user's Google account security settings.

Contact

marketing@seasidecenter.org

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's use of Google Drive changes.
